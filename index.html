Ask us anything ↗
What services does Sonam BKD offer?

We offer road transport, air freight, sea freight, moving and packing support. The right option depends on your shipment size, destination and timeline.

How do I get a quote for my shipment?

Send us your pickup and destination locations, a short description of what you’re moving and your preferred timing. Our team can then help with the next steps.

Can you help with packing and moving?

Yes. We can discuss packing and moving support for home or business relocations. Share the move size and locations so we can guide you on availability.

Which locations do you serve?

Coverage varies by service and route. Contact us with the origin and destination, and we’ll confirm what’s available for your shipment.

What information should I have ready?

It helps to have approximate dimensions or weight, pickup and delivery addresses, the preferred service and any timing requirements.
